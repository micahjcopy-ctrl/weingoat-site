Week one

You read the Playbook in an evening and run the preflop process at your next session. The first thing that changes is the hands you stop playing.

The first month

In the community you bring real hands to the weekly call and hear what the higher-EV line was, and why. The buckets start filling themselves.

After that

One on one, we go decision by decision through your sessions until "tough" spots are the ones you look for.

This is for players who already suspect there's a better way to think about the game and are humble enough to learn it. If you argue every hand, it isn't for you.
